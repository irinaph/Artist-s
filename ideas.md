# Ideas (parked)
Things outside the MVP. Each has the user problem behind it and when to revisit.

## v1.1 candidates (first after the MVP works)
- **"Share your week" card (Strava-style)** — added 2026-11-15
  - **What:** a designed card (e.g. "Week 6 complete · 38 pages · 5 artist dates") to save or share to social media. No accounts, no feed, **never any private content** (no pages, notes or reflections).
  - **User problem:** accountability (finish the 12 weeks), celebration (milestones), inspiring others to try the practice. Also a strong portfolio showcase.
  - **Watch out:** public posting can turn into pressure or comparison; this goes against the calm tone. Make sharing opt-in and milestone-based, never prompted daily.
  - **Revisit:** after a few weeks of real use. Am I actually wanting to share?

## Later

  - **Revisit:** after the 12-week self-test, if others want to use the app.

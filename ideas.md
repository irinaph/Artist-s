# Ideas (parked)
Things outside the MVP. Each has the user problem behind it and when to revisit.

## v1.1 candidates (first after the MVP works)
- **"Share your week" card (Strava-style)** — added 2026-11-15
  - **What:** a designed card (e.g. "Week 6 complete · 38 pages · 5 artist dates") to save or share to social media. No accounts, no feed, **never any private content** (no pages, notes or reflections).
  - **User problem:** accountability (finish the 12 weeks), celebration (milestones), inspiring others to try the practice. Also a strong portfolio showcase.
  - **Watch out:** public posting can turn into pressure or comparison; this goes against the calm tone. Make sharing opt-in and milestone-based, never prompted daily.
  - **Revisit:** after a few weeks of real use. Am I actually wanting to share?
- **Import notes from an export file** — added 2026-11-27
  - **What:** load a file made by "Download my notes" (US-25) back into the app.
  - **User problem:** restore after data loss, or move to a new artifact/device. v1's export is a readable backup only.
  - **Revisit:** after the MVP ships, or sooner if I ever lose data.

- **Letter to future me at the course ending** — added 2026-11-27
  - **What:** at course complete (US-24), an optional short note to myself, shown again after a set time (e.g. 3 months).
  - **User problem:** keeping the course's insight alive after week 12; a personal, reflective close.
  - **Watch out:** a timed reveal is new scope (scheduling, a new state); keep it optional and quiet.
  - **Revisit:** after I finish my own 12 weeks with v1. Did the "carry one idea forward" ending feel like enough?

- **AI shapes my ideas (beyond linking)** — added 2026-11-27
  - **What:** AI goes further than S1b's "these look like the same idea": it helps develop an idea I wrote, e.g. suggests a small next step, a way to turn it into an artist date, or variations in my medium.
  - **User problem:** goal 2, direction for creative plans: a recurring idea is found, but I don't know what to do with it.
  - **Watch out:** this is the "AI interpretation, affirmations or plans" line from the avoid list. It must work only on my idea text, only when I ask, never on pages or moods, and never sound like a coach. US-18 (idea → artist date) already covers the simplest version without AI.
  - **Revisit:** after the 12 weeks with v1. Did I have recurring ideas and get stuck on what to do with them?

## Later
- **"New pattern" card (rule-based, in-app)** — added 2026-11-21
  - **What:** when I open the app, a card says something new showed up (e.g. a theme 3+ times in a week, a mood shift). No AI, no push, from week 3.
  - **User problem:** I want to be nudged when a pattern appears, like Apple Health trend alerts.
  - **Why parked:** I write irregularly and there's no real data yet; rules guessed now would fire on noise and feed the "patterns say nothing new" quit risk.
  - **Revisit:** after the paper prototype or 3–4 weeks of real captures, to pick rules that actually mean something.
- **Bulgarian UI** — added 2026-11-21
  - **Why parked:** a second set of copy and tone work; v1 is English. Notes are already bilingual.
- **Link patterns to my creative goal** — parked 2026-11-21 (persona candidate 4)
  - **Why parked:** the goal isn't stated yet; adds a goal record + onboarding step and risks a report-card feel.
- **AI handwriting scan for richer analysis** — added 2026-11-21
  - **What:** photograph handwritten pages; AI reads them to find moods, themes and thought paths in more depth than the 10-second note.
  - **User problem:** the note only gives the headlines; I'd like to follow trains of thought across pages.
  - **Why parked:** a big build (OCR, AI, storage of private text); the market leader already does it (`research/competitors.md`); its own FAQ admits the insights can be wrong and feel judging.
  - **Conditions if revived:** opt-in, never the default; only **after week 8** (the book advises against rereading the pages early on); I pick which pages; on-device processing where possible; text deleted after analysis unless I keep it; the 10-second note stays as the light path.
  - **Positioning impact:** "the app never reads your pages" becomes "nothing is read unless you choose".
  - **Revisit:** after my 12 weeks, once I know whether the notes alone gave me enough.
- **Photos for the artist-date "what came out of it?" note** — added 2026-11-21
  - **Why parked:** storage and extra build; text first.
- **Recording weekly tasks** — parked 2026-11-15 (brief v2)
  - **Why parked:** my past attempts never got that far; the pattern capture took its place as the second core feature.
  - **Rule if revived:** own words or our own suggestions, never the book's task lists.
- **Open the app to a small group of core users** — added 2026-11-15
  - **Why parked:** the MVP is just for me; multiple users need private per-user data (accounts or equivalent).
  - **Revisit:** after my own 12 weeks; pairs naturally with the share card and the "creative cluster" idea.
- **Community (feeds, followers, comments)** — added 2026-11-15
  - **Why parked:** needs many active users, accounts, privacy and moderation, which is bigger than the whole MVP. Morning pages are private.
  - **Closer to the book:** a small, private **"creative cluster"** (a trusted group) instead of a public feed.
  - **Revisit:** after the 12-week self-test, if others want to use the app.

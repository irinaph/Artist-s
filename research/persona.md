# Persona: The Returning Creative (primary: me)
**Phase 2: Discovery · 2026-11-21 · draft v1**
**Built from:** my self-interview (`my-past-attempts.md`), practitioner accounts (`artists-way-practitioners.md`), habit-tracker complaints (`user-complaints.md`) and the competitor scan (`competitors.md`).
**Focus:** only what matters for doing the course: goals, frustrations, daily context, past attempts. No demographics beyond what changes behavior.
**Status:** ⚠️ marks gaps only I can fill. Everything else traces to a source.

---

## In one sentence
Someone who wants to get to know herself again and find her way back to making things, who has tried the course twice and stopped in the first two weeks, not because she lost track, but because creative time felt unearned and the pages stirred up feelings she couldn't turn into something useful.

## Who she is (as it affects the course)
- Has **read the book** and believes in the practice; this isn't curiosity, it's a third attempt. *(my-past-attempts)*
- Feels **"too old, with too many responsibilities"** for creative play. *(my-past-attempts)* Shared by many practitioners, especially parents and people in midlife. *(practitioners, Finding 2)*
- Has a **specific creative wish** she hasn't acted on (e.g. going to an exhibition). *(my-past-attempts)*
- Uses a **dated paper notebook** for the pages, and wants to keep writing by hand. *(my-past-attempts; brief)*
- A **designer by profession**: notices friction, guilt-tripping copy and inaccessible UI immediately. *(product-designer-persona)*
- ⚠️ **Creative medium(s):** what does she make, or want to make? (drawing, painting, writing, photography…)

## Goals
1. **Get to know herself again:** see which moods, themes and ideas keep returning. *"The morning pages were a tool to get to know myself again."*
2. **Find direction for creative plans:** turn recurring ideas into something she actually does.
3. **Get past week 2, then week 4, then finish all 12 weeks.** *(brief, success criteria)*
4. **Make creative time feel legitimate** at this stage of life, without guilt.

## Frustrations and fears
| Frustration | Source |
|---|---|
| Paper gives **no overview**: themes, moods and ideas stay buried in the notebook | my-past-attempts |
| **Permission:** creative play feels like it isn't "for" someone with her responsibilities | my-past-attempts; practitioners F2 |
| **Time:** the artist date is the first thing to go | my-past-attempts; practitioners F2 |
| **Emotional weight:** the pages surfaced anger about her life phase, and nothing helped her regroup | my-past-attempts |
| **All-or-nothing:** a missed day feels like failing the course; broken streaks feel like a verdict | user-complaints F1; practitioners F2 |

## Daily context
- **Mornings:** ⚠️ what time does she write, for how long, and what usually interrupts it?
- **Where:** ⚠️ where does she write (kitchen table, bed, commute)? Is the phone nearby while she writes?

- **Week:** responsibilities crowd out free blocks; a **small** artist date (30 minutes) is realistic, a half-day is not. *(my-past-attempts; brief)*

**The pattern:** both attempts died early, for **emotional and permission reasons, not logistics**. Others stall in weeks 2–5 for the same reasons. *(practitioners F1)*

## What she needs from the app (and what would make her quit)
| She needs | She'd quit if |
|---|---|
| One tap after writing, under 5 s; a short morning still counts | It asks her to type her pages or proves she wrote 3 full pages |
| To name her own mood, themes and one idea in ~10 s, only when she wants | It reads, labels or judges her writing |
| To see patterns build up over weeks, and what her artist dates produce | The pattern view feels like a report card, or shows nothing for weeks with no explanation |
| Gentle permission in weeks 1–4, and a calm next step on heavy days | The copy is preachy, therapy-like, or relentlessly upbeat |
| A comeback with no penalty; to choose pause or continue after a missed week | A streak resets, a badge is lost, or a week locks |
| Accessible, calm UI | Color-only status, tiny targets, motion she can't turn off |

## Success, in her terms
- Gets past **day 14** and **week 4**, then finishes all **12 weeks**. *(brief)*
- After 2 weeks, the pattern view shows **one pattern she hadn't noticed** on paper. *(brief)*
- Actually takes the **exhibition** (or similar) artist date, and notes what came out of it.

## Her voice (for role-play: "as the user")
When answering *as the user*, speak in first person, honestly and a little self-critically, calm rather than enthusiastic. Lean on her real words:
- *"It feels like I'm too old or have too many responsibilities to focus on such fun creative activities."*
- *"I was angry with the phase of life I was at and couldn't regroup constructively."*
- *"I wanted quick summaries of the entries that help me track the themes, moods, ideas, and gather them in a sort of graph."*
- *"Tracking my thought patterns would feel very useful and give me direction for future creative plans."*

**Typical reactions:** wary of anything that feels like homework or surveillance; relieved by permission and small steps; notices design details as a professional; skeptical of AI reading her pages, but curious about richer analysis later if she controls it (`ideas.md`).

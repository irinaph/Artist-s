# Paper prototype: the 10-second capture
**Phase 2: Discovery · started 2026-11-27 · runs 1–2 weeks**

## Why
The pattern view (US-12, US-13) leads with recurring ideas and switches from a plain list to grouping after **5 captures**. Both are guesses. This sets them with real data from my own mornings before Phase 3 designs the view.

## How (about 30 s, after the pages)
In the margin of the last page, only when I want to:

| Field | What to write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Date | the day I wrote | write it even if the morning was shorter |
| Mood | how the **pages** felt | a word, a symbol, or a wordless mark for "heavy" (R1) |
| Themes | 1–2 short words | either language |
| Idea | one creative idea, or nothing | |

Skip freely. A blank margin is a valid result.

## What to note after each week
1. **Capture count:** how many mornings had a margin note?
2. **First pattern:** after which capture did an idea or theme first repeat in a way I'd want to see grouped? → sets the US-13 threshold.
3. **Ideas:** on how many mornings did I write an idea at all? If ideas are rare, "ideas first" needs rethinking, not just the threshold.
4. **Heavy days:** did I mark any? Did I want to write "what's clearer now?"
5. **Language:** did the same theme appear in Bulgarian and English? (tests merge, US-14)
6. **Friction:** did the 30 s ever feel like homework? When did I skip, and why?

*Added 2026-11-27 from the fresh-eyes review (`research/synthesis.md`):*
7. **Days written:** on how many days did I write pages at all? (sets the 14-day comeback threshold, S4)
8. **Ideas away from the pages:** did ideas come on days without pages, on a commute or during an artist date? Note them on a separate line in the notebook. (tests "Add an idea any time", S2 / US-27)
9. **Same idea, different words:** did an idea come back in different words or the other language? Would I have linked them? (tests idea links, S1 / US-28)
10. **Older notes:** did I want to copy an older margin note into the app? (tests backfill, S10 / US-05)

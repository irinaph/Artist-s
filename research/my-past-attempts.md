# My Past Attempts at The Artist's Way
**Phase 2: Discovery · self-interview · 2026-11-15**
**Purpose:** test the brief's key assumption, *"Why an app, if the real work happens on paper?"* (problem #3: "it's easy to lose your place over 12 weeks").
**Method:** 6 questions about what actually happened (past behavior, not hypotheticals), asked one at a time.
**Limits:** n = 1, from memory, 2 attempts. Needs checking against other people's experiences (next research task).

## Findings

### 1. The key assumption is **not supported** ❌
I never got past week 2, so I never had 12 weeks of themes to lose track of. A dated notebook was enough to know where I was. **"Holding the 12-week path" is not why I'd need an app.**

### 2. Both attempts died in the **first two weeks**, for **emotional and permission** reasons, not logistics
- **Permission:** creative play felt like it wasn't "for" someone my age with my responsibilities.
- **Time:** responsibilities crowded out the artist date, even with a specific wish (an exhibition).
- **Emotional weight:** the pages surfaced anger about my life phase, and nothing helped me turn it into something constructive.

### 3. The real unmet need: **seeing my own patterns** ✅
Paper couldn't give me a quick overview of **themes, moods and ideas** across entries. I wanted that to **get to know myself again** and to find **direction for future creative plans**. This is the strongest answer yet to *"why an app?"*, and it's a different answer from the brief's.

## Tensions with what's been decided
| Decided (HANDOFF "don't reopen") | What the interview says |
|---|---|
| The app only records "Pages done"; it never asks what you wrote | I *want* to capture something about each entry: mood, themes, ideas |
| The book discourages rereading pages early | A short **summary I write myself** isn't rereading the pages; the pages stay private on paper |
| The 12-week path is the core of the app | My stalls happened in days 1–14; the path never mattered |

## Implications (for decision, not yet decided)
1. **Re-center the value:** from "the app holds the path" to **"the app reflects my patterns back to me."** After writing on paper, an optional ~10-second capture: mood, a theme or two, one idea. Over time, a pattern view shows how these change and points to creative directions.
2. **Keep the one-tap rule:** "Pages done" stays one tap under 5 s; the capture comes after it and is optional (same principle as the note).
3. **Design for days 1–14, the danger zone:**
   - **Permission:** gentle framing in our own words that creative time is legitimate at any age and stage.
   - **Time:** a *tiny* artist date counts (e.g. 30 minutes), like the 2-minute version in the old concept.
   - **Hard days:** a calm moment to note one constructive next step when entries feel heavy.
4. **Boundary:** the app supports a creative practice; it is **not** a mental-health tool. Calm, acknowledging copy is in scope; therapy-like features are not.
5. **The 12-week path** stays, but becomes a lighter frame, not the hero.

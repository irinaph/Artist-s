## Decided, don't reopen
- Pages stay on paper; "Pages done" is one tap under 5 s (a shorter morning counts too), then an **optional 10 s capture** (mood, themes, one idea), never the pages themselves
- **Brief v2:** the app reflects my patterns back to me (pattern view, low-key in weeks 1–2); the 12-week path is a light frame; weekly tasks are parked
- Early support runs **weeks 1–4**, tapering; heavy-day copy says an early dip is normal (2026-11-21)
- No streak resets; weeks never lock; the check-in on day 7 is invited, never required, and prefills pages and artist date
- Missed week → the user chooses pause or continue, with inspiration (our own content)
- Weekly tasks are parked; nothing in the app ever uses the book's text (tasks, check-in wording, quotes)
- Calm, reflective tone; accessibility criteria in the brief
- Course complete moment after week 12
